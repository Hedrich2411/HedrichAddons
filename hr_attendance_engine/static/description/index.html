HR Attendance Engine

Per-day shifts, punch review and payroll-ready workdays.

Odoo's attendance app measures every punch against one fixed weekly calendar. Rotating shifts, night shifts, hourly permits and holidays do not fit it. This module plans the schedule day by day, lets a supervisor clean the punches, and turns each day into a frozen workday that payroll can read: worked, late, early leave, unauthorized absence and holiday hours.

Key Features

Shifts per day

Reusable templates with breaks and tolerances, assigned to each employee day by day. Rotations, 4x2 guard rotas and shifts that cross midnight just work.

Attendance review

A grid of employees by day flags absences, open punches and incomplete pairs before anything is processed. Fix or archive punches right from the cell.

Calculation engine

Lateness and early leave with lenient or strict tolerance, break detection, hourly and half-day permits, work on rest days and public holidays kept apart for the surcharge.

Lockable snapshots

Lock the days payroll consumed: their punches, absences and holidays can no longer change underneath. Any other change sends the day back to draft to be processed again.

Absences & vacations

Absence types with codes for payroll, supporting documents, vacation periods of 30 days per year of service with sold days and expiry, as in Peruvian labor law.

Reports

Attendance, lateness, absence and vacation reports by employee, department or company, with weekly subtotals.

How It Works

Plan the shifts with Bulk Assign → review the punches in Attendance Review → generate the workdays → lock them when payroll is closed. Additional hours, paid or compensatory, are approved apart and added to the workday they belong to.
